PUMP uses the asset for the official token mint linked from https://pump.fun/pump-token. The direct Pump asset endpoint blocks automated downloads.

Other token marks are bundled in `components/ui/icon-data.ts` from the MIT-licensed [Web3 Icons Branded collection](https://github.com/0xa3k5/web3icons). `1000PEPE` uses the PEPE artwork. The collection's `lit` and `tao` marks are not used because they belong to different projects.
